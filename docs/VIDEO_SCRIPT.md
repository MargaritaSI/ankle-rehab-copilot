# Demo video — script (≤ 3 min, English, screen + voice)

Record the hosted Space (or localhost) at 1280×800. Keep the sidebar visible. No music.

**0:00–0:20 — The problem (talking over the empty app)**
"Five months ago I had cartilage transplanted from my knee into my ankle. You get a sheet of
paper, a physio slot every few weeks, and silence in between. Every question — is this swelling
normal, can I squat, how much protein — you answer alone, or a chatbot guesses for you.
Rehab Copilot answers only from published protocols and research, cites every sentence, and knows
which week you are in."

**0:20–0:35 — Profile**
Point at the sidebar: surgery type, date → "Week 22 · Strength and control". "Every answer is
anchored to this."

**0:35–1:25 — Ask (flagship)**
Click preset *Swelling after standing* → Ask. While it runs: "Nemotron Nano on Nebius Token
Factory checks the question is in scope and rewrites it for search; two tiers are searched — my
curated notes and 743 open-access papers; Nemotron Super writes the answer with reasoning on."
Show the answer: the four sections, the `[PMC…]` ids, open *Open-access papers*, click one link.
Point at the footer: "two Nemotron calls, nine seconds."

**1:25–1:45 — Honesty**
Click *Out of scope test* (shoe brands) → refused in half a second. Then type
"Упражнения при грыже" (Russian, other body part) → refused. "No guessing outside the library."
If Tavily is set: ask the caffeine question → labelled web sources.

**1:45–2:15 — Today's routine**
Tab *Today's routine* → Build. "Six one-minute items for week 22, a donor-knee item, a protein
habit, stop-signs — from the curated notes only. This is the part a physio sheet never gives you:
what to do *today*."

**2:15–2:40 — Compare + appointment**
Tab *Compare* cold vs compression → table with *no data* cells. Tab *Before your appointment* →
questions list. "Appointments are ten minutes; walk in with the right questions."

**2:40–3:00 — Close**
"Built on NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Nano, Super and Ultra via Nebius Token Factory, multilingual
embeddings from Token Factory, local FAISS, Tavily for the gaps. Open source, MIT. Not medical
advice — a calmer, sourced way through the months nobody talks about."
